# 10: Intentions

**What to build:** Phrases like "I need to…" or "next week I'll…" become intentions with the person's exact words and date. A later moment that mentions it ("called the bank today") is linked to it. Intentions go quiet after 14 days of silence, and the person can hide one; hidden does not mean failed.
