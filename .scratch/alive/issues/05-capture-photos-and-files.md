# 05: Photos and files in capture

**What to build:** The capture sheet accepts up to three photos and any work files (PDF, documents, images). They are encrypted on the device, uploaded, and shown with the moment. A storage meter shows use against the 500 MB free limit.

**Blocked by:** 04

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Up to 3 photos and any number of files per capture
- [ ] Uploaded media and files are encrypted before leaving the device
- [ ] Files show in the Vault with their capture date
- [ ] Storage meter shows MB used of 500 MB; uploads beyond the limit are refused with a clear message
