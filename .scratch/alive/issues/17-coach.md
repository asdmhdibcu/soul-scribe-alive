# 17: Coach tab

**What to build:** A Coach tab gives suggestions only when opened, each quoting the person's own words and dates (e.g. what they said they wanted and what they have mentioned since). The brief may carry at most one Coach line, with a setting to switch it off. No pop-ups or warnings anywhere.

**Blocked by:** 10, 13

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Coach content appears only in the Coach tab and at most one brief line
- [ ] Every suggestion quotes the person's words with dates
- [ ] The brief Coach line can be switched off
- [ ] No pop-up, notification or warning is ever triggered by Coach
